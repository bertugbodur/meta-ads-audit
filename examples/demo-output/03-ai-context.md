# AI context pack: Lumen & Clay Studio (demo)

> **Fictional demo.** The account, campaigns, ads and numbers below are made up to show what meta-ads-audit produces.

## Business and goal
Fictional online store selling handmade ceramics (mugs, bowls, vases). Goal: online sales, optimized for the Purchase event. Average order value about $60.28. Currency USD.

## Period
Oct 21 – Nov 19, 2026 vs Sep 21 – Oct 20, 2026.

## Structure
- 5 campaigns, 7 ad sets, 12 ads. Daily budgets total $1,500/day (active).
- SALES | Prospecting | Broad (active): $19,427 spend, 508 purchases, $38.24 per purchase, ROAS 1.55×.
- SALES | Lookalike audiences (active): $9,942 spend, 124 purchases, $80.18 per purchase, ROAS 0.68×.
- SALES | Advantage+ catalog (active): $8,949 spend, 491 purchases, $18.23 per purchase, ROAS 3.36×.
- SALES | Retargeting | 30d visitors (active): $6,581 spend, 385 purchases, $17.09 per purchase, ROAS 3.67×.
- SALES | Holiday teaser (paused): $1,377 spend, 16 purchases, $86.06 per purchase, ROAS 0.65×.

## Top 5 issues
1. Cut and consolidate the Lookalike campaign: Cost per purchase $80.18 is 2.1× the ad-set median ($38.24) while the campaign takes 21% of spend. Its three ad sets got 11, 7, 10 purchases in the last 7 days (learning needs about 50).
2. Refresh Lookalike creatives: Frequency 3.36 (previous 2.56) and link CTR fell from 1.32% to 0.99% (-24.5%).
3. Set up Conversions API with deduplication: 1,585 browser purchase events and 0 server events in the last 30 days.
4. Scale Catalog and Retargeting gradually: Catalog $18.23 (ROAS 3.36×) and Retargeting $17.09 (ROAS 3.67×) are below 0.7× the median $38.24.
5. Test excluding Audience Network: Audience Network takes 17% of spend at $68.41 per purchase, 2.3× the account average.
